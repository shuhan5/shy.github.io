ACADEMIC WORKS#

Yang, S. (2024). A Population-level Description of Theta Oscillations in the Medial Septum [Master’s thesis, University of Copenhagen].#

  • Explored the role of the Medial Septum in generating theta oscillations, crucial for hippocampal memory formation.
  • Employed PCA and CEBRA on electrophysiological data from Neuropixel probes during varied task performances.
  • Revealed rotational dynamics in Medial Septum neuronal activities linked to theta oscillations.
  • Demonstrated the ability of non-linear dimensionality reduction techniques to decode spatial information from Medial Septum neural data.

Yang, S. (2022). The P2X7 receptor: a remarkable role in Alzheimer’s disease pathology and treatment [Coursework, Higher Brain Function Course at the University of Copenhagen].#

  • Reviewed the involvement of the P2X7 receptor in Alzheimer’s disease pathology and potential therapeutic targets.
  • Summarized the impacts of P2X7 receptor modulation on alleviating symptoms and pathology in AD mouse models.

Yang, S. (2021). C9orf72/SMCR8 complex knockout effects on organelles in microglia [Undergraduate thesis, Yunnan University].#

  • Examined the link between C9orf72/SMCR8 complex absence and neurodegenerative diseases ALS and FTD.
  • Investigated the role of C9orf72 and SMCR8 in various cellular processes, including autophagy and organelle transport.
  • Utilized transmission electron microscopy to observe sub-microscopic structures of microglia organelles.
  • Identified abnormal multi-membrane vesicular structures, pointing to potential ER stress and lysosomal defects.

Yang, S. (2021). How to keep your brain clean [Coursework, Cells and Circuits Course at the University of Copenhagen].#

  • Explored the mechanisms of cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) circulation through the brain’s glymphatic system.
  • Highlighted the role of astrocytic end feet and vascular pulsations in brain detoxification.
